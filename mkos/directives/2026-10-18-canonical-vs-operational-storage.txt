==================================================
CANONICAL STORAGE VS OPERATIONAL STORAGE
ARCHITECTURAL GOVERNANCE DIRECTIVE
==================================================

PURPOSE

Clarify the role of Google Drive and D1 across MKOS, LOS, Life OS, and future operating-system projects.

The goal is to prevent operational databases from becoming the sole source of truth.

The goal is also to minimize unnecessary database growth, reduce write amplification, improve recoverability, and ensure long-term durability of knowledge and educational assets.

==================================================
CORE PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Google Drive
=
Long-Term Memory

D1
=
Working Memory

Drive stores truth.

D1 serves truth.

Drive stores knowledge.

D1 executes knowledge.

==================================================
ROLE OF D1
==================================================

D1 is an operational execution database.

D1 exists for speed.

D1 exists for querying.

D1 exists for graph traversal.

D1 exists for frontend performance.

D1 is not intended to be the sole source of truth.

==================================================
REBUILDABILITY REQUIREMENT
==================================================

A catastrophic D1 loss should not destroy the platform.

The following should be possible:

Drive

↓

Rebuild D1

↓

Restore Knowledge Objects

Restore Objectives

Restore Competencies

Restore Relationships

Restore Educational Assets

Restore Configuration

Restore Governance

Restore Audit Records

Restore Registries

Drive should contain sufficient information to reconstruct the operating system.

==================================================
SYNC REQUIREMENT
==================================================

When durable knowledge changes:

Write To Drive

↓

Confirm Success

↓

Update D1

or

Write To D1

↓

Immediately Queue Drive Persistence

↓

Confirm Persistence

The persistence operation should never be silently skipped.

==================================================
AUDITING REQUIREMENT
==================================================

Daily reconciliation should verify:

Drive Exists

Drive Is Current

Drive Matches D1

Critical Assets Persisted

Knowledge Objects Persisted

Objectives Persisted

Competencies Persisted

Relationships Persisted

Configuration Persisted

Version History Persisted

Any discrepancies should be identified and repaired.

==================================================
ULTIMATE PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Drive is the memory.

D1 is the processor.

Drive stores knowledge.

Drive stores assets.

Drive stores governance.

Drive stores history.

D1 executes against that knowledge.

The operating system should remain recoverable, portable, auditable, versioned, governed, and rebuildable even if D1 disappears entirely.
