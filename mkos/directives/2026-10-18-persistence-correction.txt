==================================================
MKOS PERSISTENCE, DURABILITY,
AND CANONICAL STORAGE CORRECTION
==================================================

The audit identified a significant divergence between the intended architecture and the current architecture.

Current State:

Drive is not acting as the canonical MKOS repository.

D1 is currently acting as the sole system of record for nearly all MKOS knowledge.

The MKOS Drive repository is effectively empty.

Restore from Drive is currently not possible.

The durability layer exists but has not actually been exercised in production.

==================================================
DESIGN DECISION
==================================================

Google Drive shall become the canonical storage layer and long-term system of record.

D1 shall become the operational execution layer.

Drive stores truth.

D1 serves truth.

==================================================
IMMEDIATE REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

Do not continue building additional registries, extraction systems, objectives, competencies, pearls, educational assets, or governance systems until persistence architecture is corrected.

The persistence layer must be verified before additional architectural expansion.

==================================================

==================================================
CLINICAL PEARL GOVERNANCE
==================================================

Clinical pearls should become durable assets.

Generate Once

Validate

Store

Reuse

Regenerate only when:

Guidance Changes

Evidence Changes

Underlying Knowledge Changes

Relationships Change Materially

Pearl Is Flagged

Pearl Is Superseded

Do not repeatedly regenerate identical pearls.
