MKOS Backend-to-Frontend Penetration Audit
Post-Ingestion Utilization, Retrieval, and Stress Testing Review

Purpose

Following completion of source ingestion, indexing, mapping, ontology expansion, knowledge-object generation, graph construction, and curriculum generation, perform a full penetration audit of MKOS.

The purpose is to verify that backend assets are not merely stored, but are actually accessible, retrievable, callable, citable, actionable, and utilized throughout the system.

Success is not the existence of data.

Success is data being reachable and useful through application functionality.

Source Documents
→ Source Registry
→ Ontology
→ Knowledge Objects
→ Knowledge Graph
→ Curriculum
→ Educational Assets
→ Assessments
→ Frontend Functionality

with all layers verified.

The objective is not merely ingesting knowledge.

The objective is proving that the knowledge penetrates through the entire system and is available to users through deterministic retrieval before AI escalation.

Target Philosophy:

Database First
Ontology First
Knowledge Graph First
Retrieval First
AI Last

Build → Index → Map → Audit → Stress Test → Validate Frontend Access → Reduce AI Dependence → Expand
