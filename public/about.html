Doctor Career Companion

A study and career development app for physicians.

Doctor Career Companion helps physicians and trainees study for board exams and plan their careers. It writes lessons, audio lessons, practice questions and flashcards grounded in a reference library of published clinical practice guidelines and board study resources, tracks progress against the exam blueprint, and has a study companion that answers questions.

It is a study aid offered by invitation to colleagues. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for clinical judgement; do not enter patient information.

Who runs it

Doctor Career Companion is built and run by one individual, the app owner, as part of the Life OS suite of personal apps. It is free and has no advertising. Contact: tpasqua3@gmail.com.

Google data

Users are not asked to sign in with Google: the app's only Google connection is the app owner's own, used to read the reference library and keep each account's study record and backups in the app owner's Google Drive. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
